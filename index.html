A sea of sand. A unit of work.

An erg is both. ERG Works is a holding company: we buy small, durable businesses, run them on their own revenue, and keep them for a very long time.

Sand turns to glass under enough heat and enough patience. Companies set the same way.

Three companies, the first held since 2016. No outside capital, and no exits planned.
